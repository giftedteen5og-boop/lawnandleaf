Our Impact

Lawn and Leaf isn't just a business — we are a youth-driven movement tackling unemployment while giving back to our community.

Youth Employment

We hire and train unemployed youth (18–35) from Ennerdale and Lenasia — creating sustainable, dignified work.

Skills Development

Every team member receives training in horticulture, irrigation, and small-business skills they keep for life.

Elderly Support

We offer free or discounted garden services to pensioners and disabled community members.

Food Gardens

We donate 5% of our profits toward establishing food gardens at local schools and churches.
